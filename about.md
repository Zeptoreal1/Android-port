# Voidius

Spam the jump button at or above **Trigger CPS** (default 8) and the assist takes over: it clicks at a perfectly steady rate lined up to Geometry Dash's 240 ticks per second. The click that starts it counts as its first click, so there's no gap. Your own clicks only keep it running. Stop spamming (or hold the button) and control comes back within about 1.5 of your own click intervals, with the button matching whether you're holding it.

## Control panel
Open it with **F5** (PC/Mac).

- **live:** your CPS, what the assist is doing right now, and a log of every takeover and hand-back.
- **speed:** trigger CPS, hold %, CPS per gamemode, go straight, quick start, match your speed.
- **modes:** tap the gamemodes it should help in (ship, wave, ufo and swing by default).
- **look:** 15 themes, glow, panel size and text size.

The pill at the top turns the assist on and off (F7 does too on PC/Mac). The first time you open the panel an animated tour walks through every control. Replay it any time with the **?** button or the **Replay tutorial** setting. **recommended** on the speed page puts every speed and mode back to the recommended setup (these are also the defaults).

## What the settings do
- **Go straight (on by default):** in ship, wave, ufo and swing it holds the height you started spamming at, so you fly flat. Turn it off for a fixed rhythm that can drift up or down.
- **Speed per mode:** ship 20, wave 15, ufo 12, swing 12, others 10 CPS by default.
- **Match your speed:** instead of fixed speeds, spam at the rate you're clicking, just perfectly even.
- **Hold %:** how much of each click is held down.
- **Quick start:** kick in after 2 fast clicks instead of 3.
- **Show CPS:** your clicks per second in the bottom-left corner, green while the assist is spamming.

Works on PC and Android (64-bit), and alongside afterflow. The panel opens with F5, so it is PC/Mac only. Panel style and Poppins fonts (SIL Open Font License) from afterflow by prsevend (MIT).
